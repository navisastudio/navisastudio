# Studio Ledger

A simple dashboard for jobs, rates, invoices, bills, designs, images and mood boards.

**Live site:** https://navisastudio.github.io

## Using it

- Open the site in your normal browser (not a private window) and bookmark it.
- Everything is saved on the device you use. Phone and laptop do not share data yet, so stick to one device for now.
- In Settings, fill in your business details once and download a backup now and then. Keep the backup file private, because it holds your bank details.
- Cloud sync and sign-in are planned, which will also bring phone access.

## Updating the app

In the GitHub repo, use Add file, then Upload files, to replace `index.html`. Your saved data is not affected.

## What is inside

`index.html` is the whole app. `vendor/jspdf.umd.min.js` makes the PDF invoices and exports (MIT licence, see `vendor/jspdf-LICENSE`).
